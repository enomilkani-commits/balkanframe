How to use Balkan Frame

What is on each page, and how to get the most out of the site.

Find your way

Balkan Frame gathers the film industry of Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Greece, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Romania, Serbia and Slovenia in one place. Use the menu at the top, or start here.

Tips

Filters

Most pages have country buttons at the top: click one to show only that country, “All” to see everything.

Links

Names with ↗ open the official website or Wikipedia in a new tab.

News ribbons

The two moving lines at the top show Balkan news and European film news. They update every morning.

Something missing or wrong?

Tell us and we will add or correct it.
Send a request ↗

Be featured

Film professionals from the region can answer our self-interview; selected interviews are published on the Community page.
Start your self-interview ↗

Always check the source

Data comes from official sources, trade press and Wikipedia. Calls, dates and rates change: confirm with each institution before applying.
